Welcome to Temixw

A school community built on belonging.

We believe learning grows through meaningful relationships—with one another, with our community and with the land around us. Our school is designed to help every learner feel included, take part, ask questions and discover their strengths.

Spaces that support learning

Room to learn in different ways.

Our proposed school design offers a range of welcoming spaces where students can focus, create, move, collaborate and connect with the outdoors.

Flexible classrooms

Adaptable furniture and learning zones support independent work, small groups and whole-class learning.

Outdoor learning areas

Outdoor spaces invite observation, movement, curiosity and learning connected to the natural environment.

Shared and supportive spaces

Resource, sensory and shared collaboration areas offer different ways to participate without separating learners from their community.

Learning in action

Curious minds. Connected learning.

Students make connections across subjects through meaningful questions and hands-on projects. A class might explore seasonal changes through science and social studies, create visual representations of their learning, and reflect on how people and the natural world are connected.

Local Indigenous perspectives are approached with respect and responsibility, using appropriate community-informed and Indigenous-authored resources rather than assuming one story or perspective represents all Nations.
