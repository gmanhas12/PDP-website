Who we are

Collaboration, community and curiosity.

Temixw Elementary is a K–7 school community designed around learning with one another and with the land around us. Our three commitments—land-based learning, inclusivity and collaboration—guide the way we teach, learn and care for our community.

Our philosophy

Learning is connected to people, place and possibility.

We believe students learn best when they feel respected, supported and able to contribute. Relationships matter: students should have opportunities to listen, share ideas, learn from different perspectives and build a sense of belonging.

Our school aims to create learning experiences where students are curious, challenged and supported. Learning happens in classrooms, shared spaces and outdoors, with connections to the local environment and community.

Our three commitments

Beliefs that shape everyday decisions

These commitments are more than statements. They influence classroom activities, school spaces and the ways students and educators work together.

01 · Place

Land-based learning

Learning should extend beyond classroom walls. Students observe the natural world, explore seasonal changes and build a relationship with the environment. We approach the land with care and respect.

02 · Belonging

Inclusivity

Every student brings different experiences, strengths, backgrounds and learning needs. We adapt learning and the environment so everyone can participate, contribute and feel respected.

03 · Together

Collaboration

Students and teachers share ideas, cooperate and learn from different perspectives. Working together strengthens relationships and builds a connected school community.

The three Cs

Collaboration, community and curiosity

These ideas bring our school commitments together and help us think about the connections between people, self and the land.

Collaboration

Making space for shared thinking, teamwork and learning from others.

Community

Building relationships among students, educators, families and community members.

Curiosity

Encouraging students to ask questions, investigate and make meaningful connections.

Spaces designed for participation

A classroom that can adapt to its learners.

Our proposed classroom design includes clear, wide pathways and movable seating to support access and collaboration. Students can work together, independently or in small groups.

  • A quiet area for reading and self-regulation that remains connected to the classroom
  • Natural light, with lamps available when additional lighting is needed
  • Duplicated smart boards to improve visibility around the room
  • Shared shelves with supplies, laptops, headphones, hand-outs and audio tools
  • Flexible furniture and accessible work surfaces
“We want every learner to have a way to participate, contribute and feel that they belong.”
