Beyond the classroom

A place to contribute, connect and belong.

Student life at Temixw is shaped by collaboration, community and curiosity. Students are encouraged to share ideas, learn from one another, explore their interests and care for shared spaces.

Everyday belonging

Different ways to take part

Students can choose how they participate and have opportunities to contribute through their strengths and interests.

Outdoor exploration

Seasonal observation, nature walks and outdoor inquiry help students connect classroom learning with the environment around them.

Shared projects

Group challenges, creative projects and classroom activities encourage students to share ideas, listen and learn together.

Movement and play

Play and movement offer ways to build friendships, practise cooperation and support well-being.

Student voice and autonomy

Students help shape their learning.

We want students’ interests, questions and voices to be heard. Choice is built into learning experiences—for example, students may select how to show their understanding through making, visual work or role-play.

Students are also encouraged to ask questions, contribute to group decisions and connect new learning to their own experiences.

A welcoming classroom

Space for different needs and ways of being

Work together

Movable seating and shared work areas make it easier to collaborate or work in smaller groups.

Take a quiet moment

A calm area can support reading, reflection or self-regulation while keeping students connected to the classroom.

Use the tools you need

Shared materials may include headphones, laptops, audio recorders, visual supports and classroom supplies.
