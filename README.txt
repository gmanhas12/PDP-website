TEMIXW ELEMENTARY SCHOOL — WEBSITE MOCKUP

HOW TO VIEW
1. Download and unzip this folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge.
3. Click the navigation links to visit the other pages.

EDITING
- Each page is a separate .html file.
- styles.css controls colours, typography, spacing, cards and responsive layouts.
- script.js controls the mobile navigation and active-page indicator.
- Search each HTML page for the sample text you want to replace.
- Replace image URLs in the HTML with your own approved photos.

PAGES INCLUDED
Home, About Us, Our Staff, Learning, Student Life, News & Updates, Events,
Calendar, Families, Student Support, Gallery, Contact Us.

IMPORTANT
This is a fictional school mockup for an educational design project. Names,
photos, address, phone, email, calendar entries, enrolment figures and all
other school details are illustrative. The contact form is front-end only and
does not send messages. Images are externally hosted from Unsplash and need
an internet connection to display.

CULTURAL CONTENT NOTE
The name Temixw and any Indigenous-related language, teachings, stories,
artwork or cultural references should be reviewed with the relevant local
Nation/community and authorized knowledge holders before public use. This
mockup intentionally keeps cultural claims general and does not invent
specific Indigenous teachings or protocols.
